Our Head Instructor

Grand Master Jung T. Jeon

7th Dan Black BeltKukkiwon CertifiedInternational Referee

Grand Master Jeon has coached Ivy League athletes, West Point cadets and the New Jersey State Team. In Chester he teaches every class himself, from four year olds to students in their seventies.

  • Former Instructor and Coach, Princeton University Taekwondo Program, Physical Education Dept.
  • Former Instructor and Coach, U.S. Military Academy Taekwondo Team, West Point
  • New Jersey State Team Coach, USA National and Junior Olympic Championships
  • Certified International Referee and USA Sparring Referee, Pan Am Taekwondo Union
