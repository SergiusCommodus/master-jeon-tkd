About the Grand Master

Decades of teaching champions

Grand Master Jeon has coached Ivy League athletes, West Point cadets and the New Jersey State Team. In Chester he teaches every student personally, from four year olds to seventy year olds.

  • Certified 7th Degree Black Belt, Kukkiwon (World Taekwondo Headquarters)
  • Former Instructor and Coach, Princeton University Taekwondo Program, Physical Education Dept.
  • Former Instructor and Coach, United States Military Academy Taekwondo Team, West Point
  • New Jersey State Team Coach, USA National Championships and USA Junior Olympic Championships
  • Certified International Referee and USA Sparring Referee, Pan Am Taekwondo Union
